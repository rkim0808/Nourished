About us

Who we are

Nourished is a student organization working to break the stigma surrounding eating disorders and to advocate for recovery.

We're a place for students in recovery, students supporting someone they care about, and anyone who wants to learn.

We focus on wellness events, community service for food insecurity, and educational sessions, to promote real wellness in our community.

Our mission

Our goal is to raise awareness about eating disorders, provide a supportive community for those affected, and promote education on healthy relationships with food and body image.

We aim to break the stigma surrounding EDs and advocate for recovery, and to engage in community service initiatives for food insecurity.

How we do it

What we focus on

♥

Raise awareness

Campus events and campaigns that help people recognize eating disorders and know where to turn.

♥

Build community

A welcoming, judgment-free space for anyone affected, and anyone that wants to support.

♥

Educate

Workshops and speakers on body image and building a healthy relationship with food.

♥

Advocate for recovery

Challenging stigma and pointing students toward care and support on campus and beyond.

♥

Service

Support our community to make sure food insecurity doesn't exist.

Support resources

If you need support

These services can help.

U-M Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS)

734-764-8312

Counseling for U-M students through University Health & Counseling. When CAPS is closed, call and press 0 to reach a mental health professional.

uhc.umich.edu

ANAD Eating Disorders Helpline

1-888-375-7767

Free emotional support and referrals. Monday to Friday, 9 am to 9 pm Central (10 am to 10 pm in Ann Arbor).

anad.org

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text 988

Free and confidential, any time of day. In an emergency, call 911.

988lifeline.org
